About Our Company

Your Trusted Partner for Premium Sumatran Arabica Coffee

Ridho Utama Berkah is an agricultural company based in North Sumatra, focused on supplying premium Sumatran Arabica coffee to international markets. We work closely with local farmers to ensure consistent quality, reliable supply, and long-term business partnerships.
