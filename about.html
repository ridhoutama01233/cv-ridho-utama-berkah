Our Story

Built to Promote the Quality of Sumatran Coffee.

Ridho Utama Berkah was established with a commitment to preserve and distribute the best agricultural resources from Sumatra, with a primary focus on coffee. Located in the heart of North Sumatra, we are close to one of Indonesia’s most respected coffee ecosystems.

We believe every coffee bean carries a story: the dedication of local farmers, the richness of volcanic soil, and the careful process behind every harvest. Our role is to connect these valuable local resources with international markets through reliable sourcing, consistent quality control, and professional export services.

As a coffee supplier and exporter, we provide green coffee beans, roasted coffee beans, ground coffee, and custom export solutions for businesses that require dependable supply from Sumatra.

North Sumatra Based

From Binjai, North Sumatra to a Wider Coffee Market.

Our company is located in Binjai City, North Sumatra, Indonesia. This strategic location gives us access to Sumatran coffee-producing areas and export logistics through Belawan Port, one of the key gateways for international shipping in western Indonesia.

With this position, we are able to support coffee buyers who need reliable sourcing, consistent product quality, and efficient distribution from Sumatra to global markets.
